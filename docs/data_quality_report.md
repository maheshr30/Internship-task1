## 4. Known limitations (worth stating in the analysis)

- **`Customer_ID` is not a reliable customer key.** 52 IDs map to more than one name, and many to different ages or genders. Treat customer-level analysis (retention, lifetime value) with caution.
- **Unit prices look synthetic.** Every product, including Rice and Book, has prices spread across roughly 150-50,000, so product-level price comparisons will not reflect real-world pricing.
- **Coverage:** 5 orders fall on 2026-01-01, just outside calendar 2025.
